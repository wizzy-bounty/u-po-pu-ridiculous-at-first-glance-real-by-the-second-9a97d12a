Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a) competition **"Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second"**: one sentence (max 200 characters) pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

## The pitch

> We rent out professional silent sitters who come to your home office and say nothing, because remote workers pay good money for a stranger whose quiet presence finally makes them work.

Paying a stranger to sit silently in your room sounds like a joke — until you remember body doubling: remote workers genuinely pay for a quiet human presence that makes them sit down and work. The absurd premise is a real market.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set). Python standard library only, no network, fully deterministic — three runs produce byte-identical output.
